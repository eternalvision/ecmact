The rules behind the sheet

The full table, the pairwise check and the list of devices that must not come back are in style-matrix.md.

  • No two pieces share more than two of the six axes. Of the 190 pairs, 140 share nothing, 48 share one axis and 2 share two.
  • Sharp corners: 1 of 20 (limit 2). Dark stages: 4 of 20 (limit 5). Monospace as the main type: 2 of 20 (limit 2). Small uppercase monospace labels: none.
  • Text keeps 4.5:1 on its background, measured from this page in Chromium: 286 boxes of text at 1280 px and 287 at 390 px were compared with the pixels behind them, the lowest is 4.69:1. Ornaments hidden from assistive technology are left out.
  • System font stacks only, no image, no web font. Shapes are CSS; a drawing that needs more is a fixed SVG string.
