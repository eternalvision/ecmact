Making things

The last small press on Cobble Street

Words by Ines Varga · photographs by Tomas Hale · 9 minute read

The shop opens at seven, when the first sheets are still warm from the dryer. Marta feeds the old machine by hand, the way she has for thirty years, and the whole room smells of ink and toast.

"A good run is one you can still afford to be wrong about."

Orders that used to be five thousand are now fifty. She has not raised her prices; she has learned to quote them honestly, down to the stock and the second ink.
